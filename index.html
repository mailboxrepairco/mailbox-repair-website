Our Story

We Started Because Nobody Else Was Doing It Right

The Mail Box Repair Co was founded by a DFW local who noticed a gap in the market: when someone's mailbox gets knocked down by a car, there's no one to call. Handymen don't specialize in it. The post office doesn't fix it. Big contractors won't touch a small job.

We built our entire business around being the fast, reliable answer to that problem — especially vehicle impact damage. Today, the majority of our calls are homeowners who woke up to a flattened mailbox, or watched a delivery truck back right over theirs. We show up same day, replace everything properly, and handle the paperwork if you need it for insurance.

When you call The Mail Box Repair Co, you're calling a neighbor — someone who knows DFW streets, respects your property, and stands behind their work.
